Optimize a prompt in any app with one hotkey, and see an estimate of how much power your computer and your AI apps are using. Everything stays on your device.

Features

Three tools in one small app

Prompt optimizer

Paste a prompt and get a shorter one back, with the tokens, energy, water and CO₂ saved. It uses the same rules as the EcoPrompt Chrome extension, so you get the same result in both.

Quick optimize hotkey

Copy text in any app, press Cmd+Shift+E on Mac or Ctrl+Shift+E on Windows, then paste. A small note shows how many tokens you saved, and you can undo for 30 seconds. It only uses the clipboard, so it needs no special permissions. You can change the shortcut in Settings.

Energy monitor

Lives in your menu bar or system tray and estimates your computer’s power use, app by app, with AI apps and browsers grouped. It keeps a 24-hour history on your computer. It is an estimate based on CPU use, not a power meter.

Privacy

No account. No tracking. Your data stays on your computer.

The desktop app does not connect to the internet. Your prompts and your energy history are stored only on your computer. (This web page asks GitHub for the latest version number, but the app itself never goes online.)

Install

First-time install: expect one warning

The warning appears because we have not yet paid Apple and Microsoft for the certificates that remove it. You only do this once.

On a Mac

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag EcoPrompt into Applications.
  2. Open EcoPrompt from Applications. macOS will say it “could not verify” the app and offer only Done and Move to Trash. Click Done. Do not move it to the Trash.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about EcoPrompt, and click Open Anyway. Enter your password, then click Open.
  4. Prefer Terminal? Run xattr -cr /Applications/EcoPrompt.app once, then open the app normally.
  5. EcoPrompt appears in the menu bar at the top right of your screen. Closing its window keeps it running there. If you can’t see the icon (a crowded menu bar can hide it behind the camera notch), open EcoPrompt again from Applications and its window comes back.

On Windows

  1. Run the downloaded -setup.exe.
  2. If Windows protected your PC appears, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Follow the installer.
  4. EcoPrompt appears in the system tray at the bottom right. You may need to click the small arrow to see it.

Want to check your download? Compare its SHA-256 checksum with the one shown above (or in SHA256SUMS.txt on the release). The full install guide has the commands and a fix for the Mac message “is damaged”.

Requirements

System requirements

  • Mac: macOS 10.13 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.
  • Windows: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Needs Microsoft Edge WebView2, which is already included in Windows 11 and recent Windows 10 updates (the installer adds it if missing).
  • Disk space: about 30 MB.
  • Energy figures: estimates from CPU use. On a Mac laptop running on battery, the app also shows the battery’s measured draw next to its estimate.
