Transparency

What EcoPrompt does with your data

Based on the extension’s source code (version 10.1.2). Read the full EcoPrompt privacy policy.

Stays on your device

Energy estimates, the sites you’ve visited (by hostname) and your history are stored in your browser. Prompt optimization runs in the extension, and your prompt text is not sent anywhere.

What is sent

When you use the Prompt Optimizer, the extension sends an anonymous usage record: a random ID, a timestamp, token and character counts, and the extension version. It does not include your prompt text or the pages you visit.

No off switch yet

In this version that usage upload is on by default and there is no setting in the extension to turn it off. Uninstalling the extension stops it.

Cost

The extension has no paid features or usage limits in its code, and it doesn’t call any paid AI service.

Energy, water and carbon figures are estimates from a simple model, not measurements. AI figures use data from the research paper “How Hungry is AI?” (Jegham et al.).
