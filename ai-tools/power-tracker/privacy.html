EcoPrompt Privacy Policy

How the EcoPrompt browser extension handles your data.

This policy covers the EcoPrompt browser extension only. For the LearnTAV website, see our main privacy policy.

Last updated: November 11, 2026. This description is based on the extension’s source code (version 10.1.2). If you run a different version, behavior may differ.

What stays on your device

To estimate energy use, a content script runs on web pages you visit (all http and https pages except the Chrome Web Store, Google account sign-in pages and Stripe checkout pages) and reads lightweight page measurements: the number of elements on the page, how quickly the page changes, how much data it has transferred, and whether it contains video or a canvas. It does not read your messages or the text of the pages you view.

The extension’s background process uses the address and title of your open tabs to label each site by hostname (for example “github.com”) and to recognize AI platforms. This information, your energy history (kept to a short rolling window plus daily totals), your display preferences and a randomly generated anonymous ID are stored in your browser using Chrome storage. The “Clear History” button in the extension’s settings deletes the stored energy history.

What is sent to a server

Prompt optimization itself runs inside the extension, and your prompt text is not transmitted. However, each time you use the Prompt Optimizer, the extension sends an anonymous usage record to a LearnTAV-operated cloud service (Google Cloud / Firebase). The record contains:

  • the random anonymous ID created by the extension, and an event ID
  • the date and time of the event
  • token counts and character counts before and after optimization, and the amount saved
  • the optimization mode, a source label and the extension version

It does not include your prompt text, the pages you visit, or your name or email address. These records are used to understand how the optimizer is used.

Your choices

In this version, the usage upload is switched on by default when you first open the extension, and the extension does not currently offer a setting to turn it off. You can stop it by not using the Prompt Optimizer or by uninstalling the extension. If this changes, this policy will be updated.

Retention and deletion

Uploaded usage records are not currently subject to an automated deletion schedule, and there is no self-service way to delete them. If you have questions or want to ask about your data, contact us at the address below.

Sharing

We do not sell this data. It is processed by Google Cloud as our hosting provider.

Cost

The extension contains no paid features and does not call paid AI services. Energy, water and carbon figures are estimates from a simple model and published research, not measurements.
