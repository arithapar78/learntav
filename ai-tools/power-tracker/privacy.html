EcoPrompt Privacy Policy

How the EcoPrompt browser extension handles your data.

This policy covers the EcoPrompt browser extension only. For the LearnTAV website, see our main privacy policy.

What EcoPrompt collects

EcoPrompt does not collect or share personally identifiable information.

How it works

The extension only analyzes website activity locally in the browser to estimate energy usage.

All data is stored locally in the user's browser using Chrome storage.

What is never transmitted

No browsing history, personal information, or website content is transmitted to external servers.

We do not sell, share, or use any data beyond the extension's core functionality.
