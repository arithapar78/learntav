Who it’s for

Curious beginners who want to make something.

“Vibe coding” means describing what you want in plain language, reviewing what an AI assistant produces, and guiding it until it works. You make the decisions; the AI does the typing.

It’s a fit for students, adults changing careers, entrepreneurs, professionals and small teams, with no coding background needed.

What to realistically expect

  • You’ll understand how to prompt and steer an AI coding assistant.
  • You’ll work on a real project of your own, scoped to fit four hours.
  • You’ll learn how to debug with AI and the basics of deployment.
  • You won’t become a professional engineer in four hours. This is a strong foundation.

Whether you finish a deployed app depends on your project’s scope and how your learning progresses. We’ll help you choose a project that fits the four hours.

Before you enrol

What you’ll need.

Your computer & setup

  • A Mac, Windows or Chromebook computer
  • A reliable internet connection
  • An up-to-date web browser, preferably Chrome
  • Access to VS Code and an AI coding assistant, where supported on your device
  • No previous coding experience required

About Chromebooks: Chromebooks can have compatibility limitations with desktop coding tools, and we can’t promise every device supports the same setup. If you plan to use a Chromebook, tell us in your enrollment inquiry so we can talk through your options before you commit.

Accounts and costs: some AI tools require an account or paid API credits. These are not included in the course price.

Course FAQ

Questions about Vibe Coding 101.

How big are group classes?

Group classes have a maximum of 5 students. Private classes are one-on-one.

Do I need coding experience?

No. Vibe Coding 101 is designed for beginners. You learn to describe what you want, review what the AI produces, and guide it toward a working result.

How much does it cost?

The group course is $99 per person and the private course is $199 per person, each for the full four-session, four-hour course. Third-party AI subscriptions, API credits, hosting and domains are not included.

Is enrolling the same as booking?

No. Submitting the form is an enrollment inquiry. We’ll email you to confirm scheduling and share payment details. Nothing is charged through this website.

Will I finish a deployed app?

Whether you finish a deployed app depends on your project’s scope and how your learning progresses. We’ll help you choose a project that fits the four hours.

Can I talk to someone first?

Yes. We offer a free, optional 15-minute introductory conversation. It’s never required to enrol.

Can I use a Chromebook?

Possibly, but Chromebooks may have compatibility limitations with desktop coding tools such as VS Code. We can’t promise every device supports the same setup, so please mention your device in your inquiry.

What tools will I use?

We work with AI coding assistants such as Claude, Cursor, and VS Code with AI extensions. Exactly which tools you use depends on your project and budget.

What if I can’t attend a session?

Tell us when you submit your inquiry and we’ll talk through scheduling when we reach out.

Ready to build your first project?

Send an enrollment inquiry. It’s not a booking or a payment. We’ll follow up with scheduling and payment details.
