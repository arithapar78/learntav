Curriculum preview

Four sessions. Four hours.

Each session is one live hour and builds on the last.

  1. Session1

    Prompting Fundamentals

    How to describe what you want so an AI assistant gives you useful results: clear goals, context, and follow-up questions.

    1 hour · live

  2. Session2

    AI Coding Tools Setup

    Install and configure an AI coding environment, such as Claude, Cursor or VS Code with an AI assistant, and learn to steer it.

    1 hour · live

  3. Session3

    Direct AI to Build

    Guide an AI assistant to build your project, then review, test and refine what it produces.

    1 hour · live

  4. Session4

    Debug & Deploy

    Use AI to find and fix problems, and learn the fundamentals of getting a project online.

    1 hour · live

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How big are group classes?

Group classes have a maximum of 5 students. Private classes are one-on-one.

Do I need coding experience?

No. Vibe Coding 101 is designed for beginners. You learn to describe what you want, review what the AI produces, and guide it toward a working result.

How much does it cost?

The group course is $99 per person and the private course is $199 per person, each for the full four-session, four-hour course. Third-party AI subscriptions, API credits, hosting and domains are not included.

Is enrolling the same as booking?

No. Submitting the form is an enrollment inquiry. We’ll email you to confirm scheduling and share payment details. Nothing is charged through this website.

Will I finish a deployed app?

Whether you finish a deployed app depends on your project’s scope and how your learning progresses. We’ll help you choose a project that fits the four hours.

Can I talk to someone first?

Yes. We offer a free, optional 15-minute introductory conversation. It’s never required to enrol.

Ready to build something real?

Send an enrollment inquiry in about two minutes. We’ll follow up by email with scheduling and payment details.
