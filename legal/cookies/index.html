Cookie Policy

How this website uses cookies and browser storage.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

What we use

This website does not set cookies of its own. It currently uses no analytics cookies and no advertising cookies.

It stores one item in your browser’s local storage, named theme, to remember whether you chose light or dark display. It never leaves your device, and you can clear it in your browser settings.

Third-party services

The website loads its typeface from Google Fonts. Some pages link to services run by others, such as Google Forms, Formspree, the Chrome Web Store, Trophy Case, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook. When you visit those, they may set their own cookies under their own policies.

Changes

If we add analytics or any other cookie in the future, we will update this page first.
