Privacy Policy

What we collect through this website and how we use it.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what LearnTAV collects through this website and what we do with it. It covers learntav.com only. Our products have their own policies: see the Trophy Case privacy policy and the EcoPrompt privacy policy.

1. What we collect

Enrollment inquiries

When you send an enrollment inquiry for Vibe Coding 101, we collect what you enter: the student’s name, email address, age range, group or private preference, prior coding experience, preferred start date, availability, time zone, optional project goals and questions, whether you’d like an introductory conversation, and a confirmation that you understand the inquiry is not a booking. The form assigns a reference number. We do not ask for payment details on this website.

Messages and other forms

If you email us, or use one of our contact, consulting or sign-up forms hosted by Google Forms, we receive the information you provide.

Technical information

Like any website, our hosting provider and the third-party services below may process technical data such as your IP address and browser type when your device connects. We do not currently use analytics or advertising trackers on this website.

2. How we use it

  • To reply to you, schedule sessions and share payment details after an inquiry
  • To run our courses and consulting work
  • To keep the website working

3. Who processes it

We do not sell your personal information. We use these services to operate the website:

  • Formspree receives enrollment inquiries and forwards them to our email inbox.
  • Google provides Google Forms (some sign-up and contact forms) and Google Fonts (the website loads its typeface from Google, which means Google receives your IP address and browser details).
  • Our hosting provider serves the website.

We may also disclose information when required by law.

4. Storage on your device

The website stores your light or dark display choice in your browser’s local storage. We do not set cookies of our own. See the Cookie Policy.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enrollment and contact information for as long as we need it to respond and run the program. You can ask us to delete your information at any time by emailing us.

6. Young learners

Our courses are open to learners of different ages, and the enrollment form asks for an age range. If you are enrolling a young learner, please have a parent or guardian submit the inquiry using their own email address. If you believe a child has given us personal information without a parent or guardian’s knowledge, email us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We rely on reputable third-party services and encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing us.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
