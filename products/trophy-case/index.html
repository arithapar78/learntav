Privacy-first design

Your achievements belong to you.

Here is how Trophy Case handles your information, in plain terms.

  • Stored on your deviceAchievements and photos are stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB.
  • No automatic cloud syncThere is no automatic cloud synchronization of achievements. The account system stores identity and session information, not your achievement library.
  • Photos are cleaned on savePhotos are resized and re-encoded when saved, which removes the original EXIF metadata (such as location).
  • Back up, restore, exportYou can back up and restore your data, and export it.
  • You can delete itYou can delete your account, and separately delete your data stored on the device.
  • AI uses a serverAI features are processed on a server. When you use them, the relevant information is sent for that request, for example your goal and the text of your achievements. Not everything always stays on your device.
  • Sign-in for AI onlyGoogle sign-in is required for AI features. Features that don’t use AI work signed out.
  • Ages 13 and upPeople under 13 can’t sign in.

No app can promise perfect privacy or security. Read the full Trophy Case privacy policy for details.

FAQ

Questions about Trophy Case.

Is Trophy Case free?

Yes, Trophy Case is currently free to use. There is no subscription or checkout in the app today.

Do I need to download it from an App Store?

No. Trophy Case is an installable web app (a progressive web app), not an App Store or Google Play app. You open it in your phone’s browser and add it to your Home Screen. Follow the steps above.

Does it work offline?

Core features that use your saved achievements can work without a connection once the app has loaded on your device. AI features (photo reading, Ranked, Scout and the profile summary) need an internet connection.

Where are my achievements stored?

On your device, in your browser’s storage (IndexedDB). Our servers don’t keep your achievements or photos, and nothing syncs to the cloud automatically. If you have an account, it holds identity and session information only.

Can parents use it?

Yes. Trophy Case is built for students aged 13–18 and for parents who want to record a young person’s wins. The app works without an account. AI features need a Google sign-in, and people under 13 can’t sign in.

Do I need an account?

Not for saving, searching and organizing achievements. An account (Google sign-in) is needed only for the AI features: photo reading, Ranked, Scout and the profile summary.

Can I export or back up my achievements?

Yes. You can create a backup file and restore it later, and export your data. The Me tab can also save a shareable profile card as an image.

What happens if I clear my browser data or change phones?

Because achievements are stored only on your device, clearing site data, uninstalling, or losing or replacing the device can erase them unless you have a backup. Make a backup file regularly, and restore it on your new phone.
